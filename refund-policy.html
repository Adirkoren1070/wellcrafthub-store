Refund Policy

Last updated: July 2026

All WellCraftHub products are digital and are delivered instantly after purchase, either as a download link or as access to an online workbook. Because of this instant delivery, we handle refunds a little differently than a physical goods store.

Standard purchases

Once a download link has been issued or workbook access has been granted, we generally do not offer refunds simply for a change of mind, since the product has already been delivered to you.

When we will make it right

We will happily issue a refund or replacement if:

  • You were charged more than once for the same order
  • A technical issue on our end prevents you from downloading your file or accessing your workbook
  • The product you received is significantly different from what was described on its product page

To request this, contact us within 14 days of purchase through our contact page, including the email address you used at checkout and the approximate date of purchase. We will look into it and respond within a few business days.

How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method through Stripe, our payment processor. Depending on your bank, this can take 5–10 business days to appear.

Questions

If anything about an order feels off, please reach out. We would rather sort it out with you directly than have you left unsure. Visit our contact page to get in touch.
